Why this take won

Post Ready picks the best delivery of each phrase across several takes. The rule is four small numbers added together. This page runs that rule on three invented takes and shows every part of every score.

What is real and what is not. The scoring and phrase-splitting code is a port of MultiTakeAssembler.scorePhrase (Post Ready 1.0.1, commit 3fff237a), checked by tests against hand-worked cases. The script and the three takes are invented for this page; no recording or real transcript is used. The window sliders change the weights for exploring only, they are not what the app uses.

What it cannot see: how a take sounds, whether a joke landed, tone. It scores words heard, fillers, pace and eye contact, and nothing else. A person still chooses what to post.
