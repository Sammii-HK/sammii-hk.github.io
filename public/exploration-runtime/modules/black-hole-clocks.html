Model & sources

A stationary clock outside a nonrotating black hole has rate √(1 − 1/u) relative to a clock at infinity, where u is the radius divided by the horizon radius. Holding the clock still requires acceleration. This is not an orbit or freefall calculation. Each local clock reads normally to its own observer.
